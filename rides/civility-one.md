## Short Description

A beautiful loop through Weston and Lincoln.

## Long Description

I adapted this ride from the old Ride Studio Cafe espresso ride, circa 2019. However, it starts in the city, goes out through Belmont, almost hits Lincoln Center, goes down Silver Hill Road, winds through some beautiful back roads in Weston before looping back through Lincoln and on to the Minuteman.

## Suggested stops

* Coffee - Barismo in Arlington Heights
* Coffee - Revival Cafe in Davis Square, Somerville
