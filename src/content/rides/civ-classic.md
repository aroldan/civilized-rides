This is one of the routes I've ridden the most. And that's for a reason: there's low traffic the entire way if you leave early, like 6-7 in the morning. By the time traffic picks up, you're on either the Minuteman or you're back in the city having a coffee. Highlights include:
- The beautiful climb up Johnson Road
- The nice climb up Grove Street
- The cheeky gravel of the Narrow Gauge Rail Trail on the way back to the Minuteman in Bedford
