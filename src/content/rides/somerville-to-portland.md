This epic ride starts in Somerville and ends up in Portland, Maine at the Bissell Brothers brewery. Take Amtrak's
Downeaster train back to Boston -- just be sure to buy a bike ticket in advance!

Highlights include:
- Riding by the Mystic Lakes and Lake Quannapowitt in Wakefield
- Rolling through the paved road in Harold Parker State Forest in Andover
- A lunch stop in Dover, NH (which also has a bike shop and an Amtrak station in case you have to bail)
- Some cool smooth gravel paths as you approach Portland
- Riding in three different states in one day
- Nachos and beers at the end, you earned 'em
