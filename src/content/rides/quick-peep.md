This is a route I made for the fall - it's good any time of year but the foliage in Belmont and Lincoln really shines in the fall.
Highlights include:
- A tough ascent from Belmont Center on Concord Ave
- Making a left after the Cambridge Reservoir onto a beautiful winding road that almost never has cars
- A quick gravel section that runs behind houses (watch for the gate upon exit)
